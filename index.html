About Icherisheher

1100+ years at the heart of Baku

İçərişəhər is the historical core of Baku, surrounded by ancient walls and one of the oldest inhabited areas of the Caucasus. In 2000, it was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List as the first site in Azerbaijan.

Within its walls stand centuries-old monuments — the iconic Maiden Tower, the Shirvanshah's Palace, ancient mosques, caravanserais, hammams and miniature museums. Today the Old City is a living neighborhood — home to more than 3000 residents who keep the heritage alive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

Do I need a ticket to enter the Old City?

No — the Old City streets are free to walk. Only museums and the Maiden Tower require tickets. Heritage Pass covers all of them.

Can I take photos inside the museums?

Yes, in most museums. Flash and tripods are not allowed. Some temporary exhibitions may have specific rules.

Is there a dress code for mosques?

Modest clothing covering shoulders and knees. Women may be asked to cover their head — scarves available at entrance.

How long do I need to see everything?

A focused 1-day visit covers the highlights. 2 days lets you add Qala reserve. 3+ days for in-depth experiences.

Are guided tours available in my language?

Yes — tours run in EN, RU, AZ, TR, AR. French and Chinese available on request. Audio guides cover 6 languages.

Our story

1100+ years at the heart of Baku

İçərişəhər is the historical core of Baku, surrounded by ancient walls and one of the oldest inhabited areas of the Caucasus. In 2000, it was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List as the first site in Azerbaijan to receive this status.

Within its walls stand centuries-old monuments — the iconic Maiden Tower, the Shirvanshah's Palace complex, ancient mosques, caravanserais, hammams and miniature museums. Today the Old City is a living neighborhood that combines daily life with its remarkable cultural heritage — home to more than 3000 residents.

The Administration of State Historical-Architectural Reserve "İçərişəhər" preserves, restores and presents this heritage to residents and visitors — through museums, audio guides, events, the official mobile app and this platform.
